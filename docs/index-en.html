Good to know

Are notifications instant?

Checks repeat about 30 minutes after a completed scan, and run right after Steam's daily price change at 10:00 Pacific time, when most sales start. Steam and Discord limits may delay delivery. Instant detection is not guaranteed.

Does it show all-time lows?

Yes, from IsThereAnyDeal’s Steam records. If a region changed currency (Turkey moved to USD in 2023), only the low in the current currency is shown, labelled “lowest since …”; prices are never converted.

Can I delete my data?

Use /delete-data in Discord and confirm. Retention and backup details are explained in the privacy policy.
