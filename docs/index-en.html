Every alert explains itself.

Each line answers something you’d otherwise open Steam to check. Depending on what Steam and IsThereAnyDeal know, an alert can include:

Your price
The price in your Steam Store region’s currency, the original price and what you save. Never converted, never estimated.
Historical low
Steam’s lowest price for your region, from IsThereAnyDeal. If your region switched currency, the low since the switch.
Worth it?
Steam’s review summary, Steam Deck compatibility and platforms, straight from the Store.
How long
When the discount ends, if Steam says so.
One tap
Open the game in the Steam app or on the website.

What Dealio won’t do.

Small promises you can hold it to.

  • Ask for your Steam password. It reads your public wishlist and nothing else. Your profile and game details need to be public.
  • Pretend to be instant. Checks run about every 30 minutes and right after Steam’s daily price change at 10:00 Pacific. Steam and Discord limits can add a delay.
  • Guess a price. No currency conversion. If Steam doesn’t answer, that’s a failed check, never a sale.
  • Message you twice for the same sale. Only when it gets clearly deeper: 10 more discount points, or 10% under a target that was already met.
  • Keep your data after you leave. /delete-data removes it. The privacy policy has the details.

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. Dealio has no paid tier. If it saves you money, the Dealio panel has a link to support it.

Where do alerts arrive?

As direct messages from Dealio on Discord. If your DMs are closed to Dealio, it pauses your alerts and the panel shows how to open them again.

Does it show all-time lows?

Yes, from IsThereAnyDeal’s Steam records. If a region changed currency (Turkey moved to USD in 2023), only the low in the current currency is shown, labelled “lowest since …”; prices are never converted.

Can I delete my data?

Use /delete-data in Discord and confirm. Retention and backup details are explained in the privacy policy.
