📬 Everything you need, right in the message.

So you don’t have to open Steam and dig around. Whatever Steam and IsThereAnyDeal know, I put in the message:

Price
The price in your own store region, the original price and how much you save. No currency conversion, no guessing.
All-time low
The lowest price the game has had in your region, from IsThereAnyDeal. If your region switched currency, the lowest since then.
Worth it?
Steam reviews, Steam Deck support and platforms, to make the call easier.
Until when?
When the sale ends, if Steam says.
One tap
Open the game straight in the Steam app or on the website.

🤝 Things I’ll never do.

A few promises, so you know where you stand.

  • Ask for your Steam password. I only read your public wishlist, nothing else. Your profile and game details just need to be public.
  • Pretend to be instant. I check about every half hour, and right after Steam’s daily price update at 10:00 Pacific. If Steam or Discord are slow, I might be a little late.
  • Make up a price. No currency conversion. If Steam doesn’t answer, I don’t mistake that for a sale; I just look again next time.
  • Pester you about the same sale. I only write again if it gets clearly cheaper: 10 more points off, or 10% under a target you already hit.
  • Hold on to your data when you leave. Just type /delete-data and I’ll delete everything. The privacy policy has the details.

💬 Questions you might have

Is it free?

Yep, completely. There’s no paid version. If I save you some money, there’s a link in the Dealio panel to support me.

Where do the messages show up?

As direct messages from me on Discord. If your DMs are closed to me, I pause your alerts and the panel shows you how to open them.

Do you show all-time lows?

Yes, from IsThereAnyDeal’s Steam records. If your region switched currency (Turkey moved to USD in 2023), I only show the low in the current currency, labelled “lowest since …”. I never convert prices.

Can I delete my data?

Of course. Type /delete-data in Discord and confirm, that’s it. Retention and backup details are in the privacy policy.
